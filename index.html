About US

Three friends—Sachin, Chetan, and Ojaswi—came together to establish SCO, a startup named after the initials of our first names. Starting with a focus on serving the local community in Thakur Village, we are now expanding our reach through an e-commerce platform, making it more convenient for customers to connect with us. Committed to delivering exceptional service, we offer innovative solutions, including ultra-fast 5-minute deliveries in collaboration with partners like Blinkit and Swiggy.
